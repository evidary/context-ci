# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be the person other people want on the project. Disagree about the work, never about the person.

## Expected

- Assume competence and good faith until shown otherwise.
- Criticise the artefact, not its author: "this fails when X and Y together" beats "this is sloppy".
- Say when you do not know. It is faster than being wrong twice.
- When you are wrong, say so once and move on. There is no prize for defending a bad decision.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, or sustained disruption of discussion.
- Sexualised language or imagery in any project space.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Dismissing a report of a problem because you did not experience it.

## Scope

Issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and any space where you are representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report to the maintainers. Reports are read by the maintainers only. Outcomes range from a private word to permanent removal from the project spaces, at the maintainers' discretion. We will tell you what we decided and why, unless doing so would expose the reporter.

## Attribution

This is a short project-specific code, not a borrowed template. It is deliberately brief so that it is read.
